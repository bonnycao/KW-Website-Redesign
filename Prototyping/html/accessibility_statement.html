Accessibility Statement

KeelWorks is committed to making its digital services accessible and usable for everyone.

Last reviewed: To be confirmed

01Our Accessibility Commitment

KeelWorks Foundation is committed to providing an inclusive digital experience for visitors, participants, volunteers, donors, and partners, including people with disabilities. We aim to make our website clear, navigable, and usable with a range of devices and assistive technologies.

02Accessibility Standards

For the KeelWorks Website Redesign, the proposed accessibility target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, subject to stakeholder approval. This is a target for design, development, and testing—not a claim that the current or redesigned website has achieved conformance.

03Our Approach to Accessibility

We aim to use clear headings and page structures, descriptive links, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus indicators, meaningful text alternatives for images, sufficient color contrast, and layouts that adapt to different screen sizes. We also aim to respect reduced-motion preferences. These features will be verified as part of website implementation and quality assurance.

04Known Accessibility Limitations

Accessibility evaluation of the redesigned website is not yet complete. We cannot currently confirm that every page, document, form, or third-party service is fully accessible. Any verified limitations and available alternatives should be documented here after testing.

05Accessibility Feedback and Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, have difficulty using any part of our website, or need information in an alternative format, please contact KeelWorks. When reporting an issue, it is helpful to include the page URL, a brief description of the problem, and the device or assistive technology used, if you are comfortable sharing those details.

06Contact Information

For accessibility assistance or feedback, please contact KeelWorks through its official website contact channel. The designated accessibility contact, email address, and expected response time should be confirmed by KeelWorks before this statement is published.

07Ongoing Improvement

We intend to review accessibility as part of ongoing website design, development, content updates, and quality assurance. Reported barriers will be reviewed and prioritized for remediation as resources and technical requirements allow.

08Statement Review and Updates

This statement will be reviewed as accessibility testing progresses and when significant website changes occur. The review date, accessibility status, and any documented limitations should be updated to reflect verified information.
